About Harborline Systems

A software and AI engineering company, built around the problem.

Harborline Systems Limited, known publicly as Harborline Systems, helps digital agencies and businesses design, build, improve, automate and maintain digital systems. The company is based in Lagos, Nigeria.

Who Harborline works with

Harborline Systems works with digital agencies, startups, small and medium sized businesses, growing companies and entrepreneurs. Agency engineering delivery is the primary offer. Direct software, AI and automation work for businesses is the second.

What Harborline does

The work covers white label software engineering, custom software, web and mobile applications, backend engineering, AI features, business automation, APIs and integrations, and ongoing engineering support. The services page describes each area, and the technologies Harborline works with.

How the work is done

  • Every engagement starts with the real situation, described in plain English.
  • Harborline frames an approach, timeline and price in writing before work begins.
  • Work follows the agreed workflow, with practical updates as it moves forward.
  • The agreed delivery is handed over with source code and useful documentation.
  • For agencies, Harborline works behind the agency brand and does not contact end clients unless the agency authorizes it. An NDA is available on request.

The full sequence is on the how it works page.

Problems Harborline aims to solve

  • Manual work repeats each day. Automate handoffs, notifications and updates.
  • Tools do not communicate. Connect systems with APIs and practical integrations.
  • A product needs another capability. Build a useful web or mobile experience, or an AI feature.
  • Existing software needs attention. Audit, fix, maintain or modernize the codebase.

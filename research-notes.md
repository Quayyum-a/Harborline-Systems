# Research notes for Harborline Systems

## Design engineering references

- [Nutlope/hallmark](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark) and its [redesign guidance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/redesign.md), [imagery guidance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/imagery.md), and [flow guidance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/flow.md): inspect the existing routes, copy and working interactions first; use real evidence and source specific image credits; redesign the composition without discarding product wiring; inspect screenshots and phone layouts; treat automated audits as prompts, not as the design outcome. Its `sloplint.mjs` can capture before/after and render actual routes if the repository and browser dependencies are available.
- [AgentsORG/design-engineering](https://github.com/AgentsORG/design-engineering): motion should be short, property specific and role appropriate; transforms and opacity are preferable for small transitions; treat reduced motion as a complete alternative; retain semantic controls, visible focus and useful touch targets. References read included `duration-table.md`, `easing-curves.md`, `prefers-reduced-motion.md`, `type-scale-and-rhythm.md`, `accessibility-baseline.md`, and `touch-and-focus.md`.
- [arham777/ui-ux-kit](https://github.com/arham777/ui-ux-kit): make brand specificity visible in multiple design dimensions; avoid default marketing composition and repetitive section patterns; use a living, actual design system; reserve image geometry, check desktop and mobile renders and verify contrast rather than guessing.
- [Krirox/anti-ai-slop-skills](https://github.com/Krirox/anti-ai-slop-skills): audit the whole page, ask whether each decoration has a product specific reason, and replace generic claims and stock proof with verifiable Harborline facts. Use its 10 dimension rubric as a manual critique, not a scanner or style prescription.
- [muris11/anti-ai-slop](https://github.com/muris11/anti-ai-slop): compose for the content, vary the structure, use type with a stated purpose, keep motion useful, and avoid unsupported claims. Familiar techniques are not automatically wrong; unexamined defaults are.
- [Payoneer homepage](https://www.payoneer.com/) was reviewed as a communication reference, not a visual template. Useful principles were a plain language promise, immediate audience segmentation, progressive explanation of complex services, conversion calls to action at decision points, and task organized product detail. Its branded visuals and composition were not copied.

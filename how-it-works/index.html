Every engagement is different. These are the details most people want to know first.

What does Harborline Systems do?+

Harborline Systems is a software and AI engineering partner. We help agencies deliver client work through confidential white label engineering, and help businesses build, improve, automate and maintain digital systems.

Do you work with agencies?+

Yes. Agency delivery is our primary offer. Harborline can work behind your brand, within your repository and workflow.

What does white label development mean?+

Harborline works as an extension of your delivery team without presenting itself to your end client. You keep the relationship while we handle the agreed engineering work.

Will you contact my agency clients?+

No. We do not contact your clients unless you explicitly authorize it.

Can I hire Harborline directly?+

Yes. Companies, founders and operational teams can approach Harborline directly for software, AI, integrations, automation, maintenance and modernization.

What technologies do you use?+

Node.js, NestJS, Java, Spring Boot, React, Next.js, React Native, Supabase, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, APIs, integrations and AI features.

How much does development cost?+

Agency capacity has clear US dollar pricing: a $750 paid pilot, $2,000 per month Half seat or $3,500 per month Full seat. Direct business work starts with a $200 automation audit or a written quotation.

Do you offer monthly engineering subscriptions?+

Yes. Agencies can purchase a Half seat or Full seat. Direct clients can choose a Care plan or Automation partner plan when ongoing support fits.

Can you build AI automations?+

Yes. We build AI assistants, document search, knowledge retrieval, AI product features and workflows that connect AI with existing systems.

Do you sign NDAs?+

Yes. An NDA is available on request.

Do you work with international companies?+

Yes. Harborline is based in Lagos, Nigeria, UTC+1, with overlap with European business hours and US East Coast mornings.

How do I start?+

Choose the path that fits, or explain the business problem in plain English. You do not need to know the technical solution first.
